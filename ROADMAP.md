# Feature roadmap

Ideas for future improvements. Priorities are a starting point, not a release schedule.

| Priority | Improvement                     | Why it helps                                                                         | Status    |
| -------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------- |
| 1        | Highlight the relationship path | Highlight compared people and connecting lines while fading the rest.                | Completed |
| 2        | Auto-arrange button             | Organize generations while keeping manual dragging available.                        | Completed |
| 3        | GEDCOM import/export            | Exchange family records with genealogy apps.                                         | Proposed  |
| 4        | Export as PNG or PDF            | Print or share a diagram without sending an editable file.                           | Proposed  |
| 5        | Richer family relationships     | Represent adopted children, former partners, and deceased status.                    | Proposed  |
| 6        | Multiple saved trees            | Switch between separate trees saved in the browser.                                  | Proposed  |
| 7        | Photos on person cards          | Upload portraits, with initials as the fallback.                                     | Proposed  |
| 8        | Collapse family branches        | Expand or hide ancestors and descendants to simplify large trees.                    | Proposed  |
| 9        | Focus on one person             | Show immediate family, then expand outward.                                          | Proposed  |
| 10       | Life events                     | Add optional birthplace, marriage date, death date, and biography.                   | Proposed  |
| 11       | Relationship explanations       | Explain cousin terms and removals using shared ancestors and generation differences. | Proposed  |
| 12       | Automatic local backups         | Restore earlier saved versions, including after reopening the browser.               | Proposed  |
