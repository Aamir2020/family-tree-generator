# Feature roadmap

Ideas for future improvements. Priorities are a starting point, not a release schedule.

## Planned improvements

| Priority | Improvement                 | Why it helps                                                                         |
| -------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1        | Collapse family branches    | Expand or hide ancestors and descendants to simplify large trees.                    |
| 2        | Export as PNG or PDF        | Print or share a diagram without sending an editable file.                           |
| 3        | Automatic local backups     | Restore earlier saved versions, including after reopening the browser.               |
| 4        | Multiple saved trees        | Switch between separate trees saved in the browser.                                  |
| 5        | GEDCOM import/export        | Exchange family records with genealogy apps.                                         |
| 6        | Richer family relationships | Represent adopted children, former partners, and deceased status.                    |
| 7        | Focus on one person         | Show immediate family, then expand outward.                                          |
| 8        | Relationship explanations   | Explain cousin terms and removals using shared ancestors and generation differences. |
| 9        | Photos on person cards      | Upload portraits, with initials as the fallback.                                     |
| 10       | Life events                 | Add optional birthplace, marriage date, death date, and biography.                   |

## Completed

- **Highlight the relationship path:** Highlight compared people and connecting lines while fading the rest.
- **Auto-arrange button:** Organize generations while keeping manual dragging available.
